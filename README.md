## 👩‍💻 A little about me

I'm a **Data Analyst** working across business intelligence, data engineering and analytics, with professional experience in consulting projects in banking and energy. I enjoy the full journey from messy source data to reliable models, useful dashboards and insights people can actually work with.

My academic background started in **Sociology**, where I discovered research methods and quantitative analysis. Since then, I've specialised in **Business Intelligence and Data Science**, and I'm currently pursuing an **Inter-university Master's Degree in Health Data Science**.

I'm especially drawn to **digital health and biomedical research**: healthcare combines fascinating analytical challenges with the possibility of making a meaningful difference.

- 💼 **Currently:** Data Consultant at Innova-tsn, working with SQL, SAS, Oracle, ETL processes, data modelling and Power BI.
- 🔍 **I enjoy:** data quality, turning complex requirements into analytical solutions, and collaborating across technical and non-technical teams.
- 🩺 **Beyond consulting:** I develop clinical analytics tools and have worked on biomedical machine learning research.
- 🧚🏽‍♀️ **Always up for:** learning something new and collaborating on interesting projects.
- 🐈 **Fun fact:** I used to cry over maths in elementary school. Now I work with data for a living. Life is weird.


## 🩺 Digital health & research

### VIDAT | Clinical Analytics & Decision Support
*2026 – Present*

I lead the technical development of **VIDAT's data-driven clinical decision-support platform**, created to make cardiometabolic information easier for healthcare professionals to interpret and discuss with patients during preventive care consultations.

- Design the data architecture, transformation workflows and semantic models linking clinical inputs to reporting.
- Build rule-based clinical indicators and cardiometabolic assessments using **Power Query, DAX and Power BI**.
- Develop patient-specific dashboards to help clinicians visualise measurements, risk factors and follow-up information.
- Implement scenario-based analyses showing estimated changes in selected cardiometabolic risk factors.

### Clarity Stethoscope | AI & Respiratory Sound Analysis
*2020 – 2024*

I co-founded [Clarity Stethoscope](https://www.stethoscope-clarity.com/), a digital health project exploring **remote respiratory auscultation and machine learning for respiratory sound analysis**.

- Processed biomedical audio and extracted signal features using **MFCC and Wavelet analysis**.
- Trained and evaluated supervised classification models and contributed to **preclinical validation studies**.
- Collaborated with clinical, technical and regulatory profiles throughout product development.

Our work led to abstracts published in the *European Respiratory Journal* and presented at the ERS International Congress. The project also received recognition in the Catalan entrepreneurship ecosystem.
